About Used Cars Philippines

I'm a car agent based in the Philippines, connecting buyers with quality pre-owned vehicles across Metro Manila and Mindanao.

My Story

It all started about 5 years ago when I helped sell our family's cars. That experience taught me the ins and outs of the used car market — how to price a vehicle, what buyers look for, and how to close a deal that both parties are happy with.

About 2 years ago, I turned that experience into a full-time service as an agent. Today, I help car owners find buyers and help buyers find the right car at the right price. I don't own the cars myself — I work directly with the owners to get you the best deal possible.

How I Work

When a car owner wants to sell their vehicle, they give me the details — brand, model, year, price, and condition. I then list it on this website and work to find a serious buyer.

When a buyer shows interest, I coordinate between both parties. I help negotiate the price and make sure everything is clear before the deal. I only earn a commission when the sale is successfully completed — so my goal is always to make the deal happen.

How Commission Works

As an agent, I charge a commission fee from the car owner when I successfully sell their vehicle. The commission is agreed upon upfront before listing. Payment methods include:

  • GCash — Fast and convenient digital transfer
  • Bank Transfer — Direct to your bank account
  • Cash — In-person payment during the deal

Will I Be Present at the Deal?

If the location allows, I can be physically present during the transaction to help facilitate the deal and ensure everything goes smoothly. For deals in areas I can't reach (like Mindanao), I coordinate everything remotely and you deal directly with the owner — but I've already done the groundwork to make sure both parties are comfortable and the terms are clear.

Areas I Cover

I primarily operate in Metro Manila, but I also handle deals in Mindanao and can coordinate for other areas as well. Distance is not a barrier — I've successfully closed deals remotely and can do the same for you.
